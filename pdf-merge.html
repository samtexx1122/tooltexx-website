Merge PDF Files

Add two or more PDFs, arrange them in the order you want, and ToolTexx stitches them into one combined PDF file you can download immediately.

How to use the Merge PDF Files

  1. Open the tool and add the PDF files you want to combine.
  2. Arrange the files in the order they should appear in the final document.
  3. Merge and download the combined PDF.

Features

  • Combine any number of PDFs into a single file.
  • Files are processed locally in your browser.
  • The merged file keeps ToolTexx's standard naming so you can tell it apart from your originals.

Supported formats

Input: multiple PDF files. Output: one merged PDF.

Honest limitations

Very large or heavily image-based PDFs may take longer to process since everything runs on your device rather than a server.

Privacy

This tool processes your file or text locally in your browser. It is not uploaded to ToolTexx's servers as part of the conversion. See the Privacy Policy for full details, including information about analytics and advertising.

FAQ

Can I change the order of the files before merging?

Yes, add the files in the order you want them to appear in the final PDF.

Is there a limit to how many PDFs I can merge?

You can merge multiple files; extremely large batches may be slower because processing happens in your browser.
