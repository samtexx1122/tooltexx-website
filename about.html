About ToolTexx

ToolTexx is a free collection of 20 everyday tools for working with PDFs, images, audio and text — all built to run directly in your web browser.

Why we built it

Most online file-conversion sites upload your files to a server, process them remotely, and store copies you have no control over. ToolTexx takes a different approach: every tool on this site — PDF merging, image compression, audio conversion, and everything else — runs entirely on your own device using JavaScript. Your files are never uploaded anywhere.

What you can do here

  • Convert, merge, split and compress PDF files
  • Compress, resize, crop and convert images
  • Convert and compress audio files
  • Generate QR codes and secure passwords
  • Format JSON, encode/decode Base64, count words, convert units, and more

Who it's for

ToolTexx is built for anyone who needs a quick, no-signup way to handle common file tasks — students, freelancers, office workers, and anyone who wants a fast tool without installing software or creating an account.
