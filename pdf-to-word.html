PDF to Word Converter

This tool reads the position, font, and size of every piece of text in your PDF — plus any images — and rebuilds it as a Word document: tables, timesheets, invoices and forms become real Word tables; bold, italic, and heading-sized text are preserved; and images are carried over too. Everything happens locally in your browser tab, so the PDF itself never leaves your device.

How to use the PDF to Word Converter

  1. Tap “Open Tool” (or “Choose File”) and select the PDF you want to convert.
  2. Wait a moment while ToolTexx reads the pages and rebuilds the layout.
  3. Download the generated .docx file once the status shows “Done.”

Features

  • Reconstructs table and grid layouts (timesheets, invoices, forms) as real Word tables, not flattened text.
  • Preserves bold and italic emphasis, and renders notably larger text as headings.
  • Extracts images from the PDF and places them in the document at roughly the right point in the text.
  • Detects genuine two-column layouts (like a resume) and reads them in the correct left-column-then-right-column order.
  • Runs entirely in your browser — the PDF is never uploaded to a server.
  • Works on PDFs up to the size limit shown in the tool.
  • Output keeps the original filename, with “ToolTexx converted” added before the extension.

Supported formats

Input: PDF. Output: .docx (Word).

Honest limitations

Table, formatting, and image detection are all based on analyzing the PDF's text and drawing instructions, so results are very good for most documents but not pixel-perfect for every layout. Exact fonts, precise colors, page backgrounds, and exact image positioning are not carried over — the focus is on getting the text, structure, emphasis, and images right, not a visual copy of the PDF.

Privacy

This tool processes your file or text locally in your browser. It is not uploaded to ToolTexx's servers as part of the conversion. See the Privacy Policy for full details, including information about analytics and advertising.

FAQ

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. The conversion runs locally in your browser using JavaScript, so the file stays on your device.

Will tables and columns line up the way they do in the PDF?

In most cases, yes — the tool detects rows and columns from the PDF and rebuilds them as a real Word table. Very unusual or densely nested layouts may not line up perfectly.
