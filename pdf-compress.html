Compress PDF

ToolTexx rebuilds your PDF's internal structure to cut down on unnecessary overhead, which can shrink the file somewhat without a server round-trip.

How to use the Compress PDF

  1. Open the tool and upload the PDF you want to shrink.
  2. Let ToolTexx rebuild the file.
  3. Download the compressed PDF and compare the file size.

Features

  • No upload — compression happens locally in your browser.
  • Keeps your original filename, with ToolTexx's naming convention appended.

Supported formats

Input: PDF. Output: compressed PDF.

Honest limitations

Savings are modest and depend heavily on how the PDF was built. Embedded images are not recompressed, so PDFs that are mostly scanned images or photos will shrink far less than text-heavy PDFs.

Privacy

This tool processes your file or text locally in your browser. It is not uploaded to ToolTexx's servers as part of the conversion. See the Privacy Policy for full details, including information about analytics and advertising.

FAQ

How much smaller will my PDF get?

It depends on the file. Text-heavy PDFs with structural overhead see the best results; PDFs made mostly of large images will shrink very little since images aren't recompressed.
