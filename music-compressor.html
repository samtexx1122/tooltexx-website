Music Compressor

Reduce an audio file's size by re-encoding it at a lower bitrate, which is handy for saving storage space or making a file quicker to send.

How to use the Music Compressor

  1. Open the tool and choose your audio file.
  2. Let ToolTexx re-encode it at a lower bitrate.
  3. Download the smaller file.

Features

  • Shrinks audio file size.
  • Processed entirely in your browser.

Supported formats

Output: compressed MP3.

Honest limitations

Lower bitrates reduce audio quality — there's a tradeoff between file size and fidelity.

Privacy

This tool processes your file or text locally in your browser. It is not uploaded to ToolTexx's servers as part of the conversion. See the Privacy Policy for full details, including information about analytics and advertising.
